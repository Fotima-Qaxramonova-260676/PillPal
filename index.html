Why this project exists

A school. A madrasa. A place for girls to learn.

School Madrasa Balkh is being developed as an educational project for children, with a particular focus on creating opportunities for Afghan girls to study.

The project is designed around a simple idea: education needs a real place — classrooms, learning spaces, basic infrastructure and an environment where students can build knowledge and confidence.

The construction budget is currently estimated at $780,000. The website will show the fundraising target, the amount received, the remaining amount and published project updates.
